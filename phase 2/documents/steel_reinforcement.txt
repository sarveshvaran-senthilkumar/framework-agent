STEEL REINFORCEMENT SPECIFICATION - SECTION 300: REINFORCING STEEL FOR CONCRETE

1.0 MATERIAL GRADES AND YIELD STRENGTH
1.1 All high-yield strength deformed (TMT) bars shall conform to IS 1786 Grade Fe 500D with a minimum characteristic yield strength of 500 N/mm².
1.2 Where specified for heavy load structural members, Grade Fe 550D reinforcement shall be used having a minimum yield strength of 550 N/mm².
1.3 The minimum percentage elongation for Fe 500D steel rebar at fracture shall not be less than 16.0 percent.
1.4 The ultimate tensile strength to yield strength ratio (TS/YS) for seismic-resistant rebar must be at least 1.25.

2.0 LAP LENGTHS AND SPLICE REQUIREMENTS
2.1 Tension lap length for Fe 500D rebar in M25 concrete shall be a minimum of 41 times the bar diameter (41d).
2.2 Compression lap length for structural steel reinforcement shall not be less than 24 times the bar diameter (24d).
2.3 The maximum proportion of main reinforcement lap spliced at any single section location shall not exceed 50 percent of the total bars.
2.4 Lap splices shall not be located within a distance of 2 times the member depth from the face of column-beam joints.
2.5 Mechanical couplers must develop at least 125 percent of the specified characteristic yield strength of the rebar.

3.0 BAR BENDING AND FABRICATION
3.1 Internal bend diameter for 90-degree standard hooks in main tension rebar shall be a minimum of 4 times the bar diameter (4d) for bars up to 25mm.
3.2 Standard 135-degree seismic stirrup hooks shall feature a straight extension length of at least 10 times the bar diameter (10d) or 75 mm, whichever is greater.
3.3 Minimum bend diameter for cold bending of reinforcement bars of diameter greater than 25mm shall be at least 6 times the bar diameter (6d).
3.4 Rebar bending rate shall be maintained within control without heating, and field re-bending of embedded bars shall not exceed a maximum angle of 45 degrees.

4.0 CONCRETE COVER REQUIREMENTS
4.1 The nominal clear concrete cover for foundation footings in direct contact with soil shall be a minimum of 50 millimeters.
4.2 Clear concrete cover for structural columns subjected to moderate weather exposure shall be at least 40 millimeters.
4.3 Clear concrete cover for structural beams shall be a minimum of 25 millimeters or bar diameter, whichever is greater.
4.4 Clear concrete cover for floor slabs shall be a minimum of 15 millimeters or bar diameter, whichever is greater.
4.5 Tolerance on specified concrete cover dimensions shall not exceed +/- 5 millimeters.

5.0 REBAR SPACING AND PLACEMENT
5.1 Clear horizontal distance between parallel main reinforcing bars shall be at least the largest bar diameter or 25 millimeters, whichever is greater.
5.2 Maximum clear spacing between main tension reinforcing bars in beams shall not exceed 180 millimeters for Fe 500 steel.
5.3 Maximum clear spacing between shear stirrups in beams shall not exceed 0.75 times the effective depth or 300 millimeters, whichever is smaller.
5.4 Minimum diameter of shear stirrup ties in structural beams and columns shall be at least 8 millimeters.
