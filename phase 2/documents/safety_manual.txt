CONSTRUCTION SITE SAFETY MANUAL - SECTION 1000: HEALTH, SAFETY AND ENVIRONMENT (HSE)

1.0 PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT (PPE)
1.1 All site personnel and visitors must wear industrial safety helmets conforming to IS 2925 at all times within active construction zones.
1.2 Workers operating at heights exceeding 2.0 meters above ground level shall wear full-body safety harnesses equipped with dual lanyards and shock absorbers complying with IS 3521.
1.3 Safety harness anchorage points shall be capable of sustaining a minimum static load of at least 22 kN (2,250 kg) per connected worker.
1.4 High-visibility reflective safety vests complying with Class 2 retroreflective standards must be worn by all site personnel.
1.5 Steel toe-cap safety boots with anti-puncture mid-soles capable of withstanding minimum 200 Joules impact force shall be worn by all active site workers.
1.6 Ear defenders or earplugs must be provided and worn in areas where continuous noise exposure levels exceed a maximum threshold of 85 dBA.

2.0 SCAFFOLDING AND WORK AT HEIGHTS
2.1 All tubular steel scaffolding structures shall be inspected by a competent safety officer at least once every 7 days and after any adverse weather event.
2.2 Scaffold platform working decks shall have a clear minimum width of at least 600 millimeters for general access, and 900 mm for heavy material storage.
2.3 Guardrails on scaffold working platforms shall consist of a top rail mounted at a height of minimum 1.0 meter to maximum 1.15 meters above the deck.
2.4 An intermediate mid-rail must be installed at a height of 500 millimeters above the platform surface.
2.5 Scaffold toe-boards with a minimum vertical height of at least 150 millimeters shall be installed along all open edges of working platforms.
2.6 Access ladders on scaffolding towers shall be inclined at an angle of 75 degrees to the horizontal (4:1 height-to-base ratio).

3.0 EXCAVATION AND TRENCH SAFETY
3.1 Excavation pits or trenches exceeding a depth of 1.5 meters must be provided with certified shoring, benching, or sloping systems.
3.2 Excavated soil spoils and heavy construction machinery shall be set back at a minimum clear distance of at least 1.5 meters from the excavation trench edge.
3.3 Trench ladders or ramps for egress shall be provided within a maximum lateral travel distance of not more than 7.5 meters for workers in trenches.
3.4 Heavy rigid safety barricades of minimum height 1.0 meter with yellow/black reflective warning tape shall encircle all open excavation pits.

4.0 FIRE PROTECTION AND FIRST AID
4.1 Portable ABC dry chemical powder fire extinguishers of minimum 6.0 kg capacity shall be positioned at a maximum walking distance of not more than 15 meters from any point on active floors.
4.2 At least one portable 4.5 kg CO2 fire extinguisher shall be installed adjacent to main electrical distribution panels.
4.3 Every site work zone shall maintain a fully stocked first aid kit capable of treating at least 20 workers per 50-person crew block.
4.4 Hot work permits must be issued for all welding and cutting operations, requiring a mandatory fire watch officer for a minimum of 30 minutes following completion of work.
4.5 Emergency evacuation route signage with emergency lighting shall be maintained along all corridors with a minimum clear passage width of at least 1.2 meters.
