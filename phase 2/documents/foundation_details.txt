SUBSTRUCTURE AND FOUNDATION SPECIFICATION - SECTION 700: FOUNDATION ENGINEERING

1.0 EXCAVATION AND EMBANKMENT
1.1 Excavation for isolated footings shall extend to a minimum depth of 1.5 meters below natural ground level (NGL) or hard strata.
1.2 Excavation side slopes in ordinary cohesive soils without shoring shall not exceed a maximum inclination of 1:1 (45 degrees).
1.3 Deep excavation pits exceeding 3.0 meters depth must be stabilized using active temporary strutting or soldier pile retaining walls.
1.4 Over-excavation beyond foundation design level shall be backfilled with M10 grade mass concrete up to the designated invert elevation; soil backfill is strictly prohibited under footing bases.
1.5 Compaction of soil backfill around foundation structures shall achieve a minimum dry density of at least 95 percent of maximum laboratory dry density (Proctor density).

2.0 SOIL BEARING CAPACITY AND SETTLEMENT
2.1 Safe bearing capacity (SBC) of soil beneath shallow foundations shall be confirmed by plate load test for a minimum value of 150 kN/m² at founding level.
2.2 Total allowable settlement for isolated column footings on sandy gravel soils shall not exceed maximum 50 millimeters.
2.3 Differential settlement between adjacent column footings shall not exceed maximum 0.002 times the span distance (L / 500).
2.4 Total allowable settlement for raft foundations on clay soils shall not exceed a maximum limit of 75 millimeters.

3.0 PLAIN CONCRETE BEDDING (PCC) AND FOOTING DIMENSIONS
3.1 A sub-base levelling course of Plain Cement Concrete (PCC) of minimum grade M15 shall be provided under all reinforced concrete footings.
3.2 Minimum thickness of the PCC bedding layer under RCC footings shall be at least 100 millimeters.
3.3 The PCC levelling bed shall project laterally beyond the outer edge of the RCC footing by a minimum offset of 100 millimeters on all sides.
3.4 Minimum thickness of RCC isolated footing edges resting on soil shall not be less than 150 millimeters.
3.5 Minimum edge thickness of sloped or stepped RCC footings resting on PCC bed shall be at least 200 millimeters.

4.0 CONCRETE GRADE AND REINFORCEMENT
4.1 Reinforced concrete for foundation elements in contact with ground soil shall use a minimum concrete grade of M25.
4.2 Where soil analysis indicates soluble sulphate concentrations exceeding 0.2 percent, sulphate-resisting Portland cement (SRC) shall be used with a minimum cement content of at least 330 kg/m³.
4.3 Clear concrete cover for foundation reinforcement bars placed directly against PCC bed shall be a minimum of 50 millimeters.
4.4 Clear concrete cover for foundation steel placed directly against earth without PCC bed shall be at least 75 millimeters.
4.5 Minimum percentage of reinforcement in isolated footings in both orthogonal directions shall be at least 0.12 percent of total cross-sectional area.

5.0 WATERPROOFING AND SUB-DRAINAGE
5.1 Exterior surfaces of basement foundation walls below grade level shall receive a minimum 2-coat cold-applied elastomeric bituminous waterproofing membrane with a minimum dry film thickness of 2.0 millimeters.
5.2 Sub-surface perimeter French drain pipes of minimum 100 mm diameter perforated PVC shall be installed surrounded by a minimum 150 mm thick filter aggregate envelope.
