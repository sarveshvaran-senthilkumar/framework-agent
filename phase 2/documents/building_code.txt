NATIONAL BUILDING CODE COMPLIANCE SPECIFICATION - SECTION 100: GENERAL BUILDING REQUIREMENTS

1.0 SCOPE AND APPLICABILITY
This specification defines the minimum dimensional, structural, and environmental standards for residential and commercial building construction in accordance with NBC 2016.

2.0 HABITABLE ROOM DIMENSIONS
2.1 The clear ceiling height for all habitable rooms shall be a minimum of 2.75 meters, measured from the finished floor surface to the lowest point of the ceiling slab.
2.2 The minimum area of any single habitable room in a residential building shall not be less than 9.5 square meters.
2.3 The minimum width of any habitable room shall be at least 2.4 meters.
2.4 Kitchen areas shall have a minimum floor area of at least 5.0 square meters with a minimum clear width of 1.8 meters.
2.5 The height of kitchen ceilings shall be a minimum of 2.6 meters from finished floor level.

3.0 LIGHTING AND VENTILATION
3.1 Every habitable room shall feature aggregate opening/window areas of not less than 10 percent of the total floor area.
3.2 Water closets and bathrooms shall have a minimum window or ventilator area of at least 0.37 square meters opening directly to external air or light shaft.
3.3 The minimum cross-sectional area of any vertical ventilation shaft serving bathrooms shall not be less than 1.2 square meters, with a minimum dimension of 0.9 meters.

4.0 CIRCULATION AND STAIRCASE DESIGN
4.1 The minimum clear width of staircases in residential buildings shall be at least 1.0 meter.
4.2 For commercial and public assembly buildings, the staircase clear width shall not be less than 1.5 meters.
4.3 The maximum riser height for residential staircases shall not exceed 150 millimeters.
4.4 The minimum tread width for residential staircases shall be at least 250 millimeters, exclusive of nosing.
4.5 Handrails shall be provided at a minimum height of 900 millimeters measured vertically from the center of the tread.
4.6 The clear head-room measured vertically above any staircase tread landing shall be a minimum of 2.2 meters.

5.0 SETBACKS AND SITE PLANNING
5.1 The minimum front setback for buildings up to 12 meters in height shall be at least 3.0 meters from the property boundary line.
5.2 The minimum rear setback shall not be less than 2.0 meters for building heights up to 10 meters.
5.3 Side setbacks shall be a minimum of 1.5 meters on each side for detached residential structures.
5.4 The Floor Area Ratio (FAR) for standard residential plots shall not exceed 2.25.
5.5 Ground coverage for residential plots above 500 square meters shall not exceed maximum 60 percent of total plot area.

6.0 PARKING PROVISIONS
6.1 Off-street parking shall be provided at a minimum rate of 1 Equivalent Car Space (ECS) per 80 square meters of residential carpet area.
6.2 The minimum dimensions for a standard car parking bay shall be at least 2.5 meters in width by 5.0 meters in length.
6.3 Driveways providing access to parking bays shall maintain a minimum clear width of 3.5 meters for one-way traffic and at least 6.0 meters for two-way traffic.
