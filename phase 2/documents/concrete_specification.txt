CONCRETE STRUCTURE SPECIFICATION - SECTION 200: CAST-IN-PLACE CONCRETE

1.0 MATERIALS AND COMPRESSIVE STRENGTH
1.1 Plain Concrete Foundation (PCC) shall use a minimum concrete grade of M15 having a minimum 28-day characteristic compressive strength of 15 N/mm².
1.2 Reinforced Concrete (RCC) structural elements shall utilize a minimum concrete grade of M25 with a minimum 28-day characteristic compressive strength of 25 N/mm².
1.3 Structural columns in high-rise sections shall require a minimum grade of M30 concrete achieving at least 30 N/mm² compressive strength at 28 days.
1.4 The 7-day compressive strength of structural concrete must achieve at least 67 percent of the required 28-day characteristic compressive strength.

2.0 WATER-CEMENT RATIO AND MIX DESIGN
2.1 The maximum free water-cement ratio for RCC work under moderate exposure conditions shall not exceed 0.50.
2.2 The maximum free water-cement ratio for concrete subjected to severe exposure conditions shall not exceed 0.45.
2.3 Minimum cement content for M25 grade RCC under moderate exposure shall be at least 300 kg/m³.
2.4 Total cementitious material content in structural concrete mixes shall not exceed maximum 450 kg/m³ to limit heat of hydration.
2.5 The minimum mixing time for batching concrete in a mechanical mixer shall not be less than 90 seconds after all dry ingredients and water are loaded.

3.0 WORKABILITY AND SLUMP REQUIREMENTS
3.1 Concrete placed in shallow foundations and heavily reinforced slabs shall maintain a slump value of at least 50 mm and maximum 75 mm at point of discharge.
3.2 Concrete intended for pumped placement in beams and columns shall maintain a slump value between minimum 100 mm and maximum 125 mm.
3.3 Concrete for tremie placement in deep bored piles must maintain a slump range of 150 mm to 200 mm.

4.0 ADMIXTURES AND CHEMICAL ADDITIVES
4.1 Polycarboxylate ether (PCE) based superplasticizer dosage shall not exceed 2.0 percent by weight of total cementitious content.
4.2 Mineral admixtures such as fly ash substitution shall not exceed maximum 35 percent of total cementitious material by weight.
4.3 Ground Granulated Blast-Furnace Slag (GGBS) substitution in marine concrete shall be at least 40 percent and maximum 70 percent of cementitious content.
4.4 Calcium chloride or chloride-based accelerating admixtures shall strictly not be used in any reinforced concrete structure.

5.0 CURING AND PROTECTION
5.1 Moist curing of ordinary Portland cement (OPC) concrete surfaces shall be maintained continuously for a minimum period of 7 days.
5.2 Concrete incorporating fly ash, GGBS, or mineral blends shall be cured for a minimum period of at least 10 days under standard conditions.
5.3 In hot weather conditions (above 40°C), curing duration must be extended to a minimum of 14 days.
5.4 Concrete temperature at the time of placement shall not exceed maximum 32°C.
5.5 Initial curing spray or wet hessian wrapping must commence within maximum 30 minutes after initial set of concrete.
