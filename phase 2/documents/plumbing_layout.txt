PLUMBING AND SANITARY SPECIFICATION - SECTION 900: PLUMBING AND DRAINAGE SYSTEMS

1.0 PIPE MATERIALS AND PRESSURE RATINGS
1.1 Cold water domestic supply piping inside buildings shall use CPVC pipes conforming to IS 15778 with a minimum pressure rating of SDR 11 (28 kg/cm²).
1.2 Hot water supply lines must utilize Class 1 CPVC pipes rated for continuous operating temperatures up to a minimum of 82°C.
1.3 Underground soil and waste drainage lines outside the building footprint shall be constructed using UPVC pipes of minimum stiffness class SN4 (IS 4985).
1.4 Overhead cold water supply distribution mains shall maintain a minimum working pressure of at least 1.5 bar (150 kPa) at the highest fixture branch.
1.5 Maximum water static pressure at any sanitary plumbing fixture outlet shall not exceed 4.0 bar (400 kPa) to prevent water hammer.

2.0 PIPE GRADIENTS AND DRAINAGE SLOPES
2.1 Soil drainage pipes of 100 mm diameter carrying water closet discharge shall be laid at a minimum gradient slope of 1 in 60 (1.67 percent).
2.2 Waste drainage pipes of 75 mm diameter serving sinks and floor drains shall be laid at a minimum slope of 1 in 50 (2.0 percent).
2.3 Main horizontal building underground sewer collector pipes of 150 mm diameter shall be laid at a minimum gradient of 1 in 100 (1.0 percent).
2.4 Soil and waste pipe flow velocity in horizontal runs shall maintain a minimum self-cleansing velocity of 0.75 meters per second.
2.5 Maximum flow velocity in drainage lines shall not exceed 2.5 meters per second to prevent scouring and erosion of pipe invert.

3.0 TRAP REQUIREMENTS AND WATER SEALS
3.1 Every floor drain trap (nahani trap or P-trap) shall maintain a minimum water seal depth of at least 50 millimeters.
3.2 Water closet soil outlets must feature an integrated P-trap or S-trap with a minimum water seal depth of not less than 50 millimeters.
3.3 Kitchen sink waste outlets shall be fitted with a detachable bottle trap having a minimum liquid seal depth of 75 millimeters.
3.4 Gully traps installed in external yards shall feature a minimum water seal depth of at least 65 millimeters.

4.0 VENT PIPES AND STACK DESIGN
4.1 The primary soil stack vent pipe terminating above roof level shall extend to a minimum height of 1.0 meter above the roof finish surface.
4.2 Minimum diameter of vertical soil stack pipes serving more than 3 water closets shall be at least 100 millimeters.
4.3 Minimum diameter of vertical anti-siphonage vent pipes connected to soil stacks shall be at least 50 millimeters.
4.4 The offset angle for horizontal branch connections to vertical soil stacks shall not be less than 45 degrees to the horizontal.

5.0 FIXTURE DISTANCES AND CLEARANCES
5.1 Clear space in front of water closets measured from the front edge of the bowl to opposite wall or door shall be a minimum of 600 millimeters.
5.2 Centerline spacing between adjacent wash basins installed in a row shall be at least 750 millimeters.
5.3 Wall-hung wash basins shall be installed with the rim at a finished height of minimum 800 mm and maximum 850 mm above finished floor level.
5.4 Minimum clear distance between the center of a water closet bowl and any side wall or partition shall be at least 380 millimeters.
