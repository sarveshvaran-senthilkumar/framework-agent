REINFORCED CONCRETE COLUMN DESIGN SPECIFICATION - SECTION 500: STRUCTURAL COLUMNS

1.0 GEOMETRIC CONSTRAINTS AND SLENDERNESS RATIO
1.1 The minimum cross-sectional dimension of any structural column shall not be less than 200 millimeters for non-seismic frames.
1.2 For columns forming part of seismic force resisting frames, the minimum dimension shall be at least 300 millimeters.
1.3 The ratio of shortest cross-sectional dimension to longest dimension of a rectangular column shall not be less than 0.40.
1.4 The unsupported length to least lateral dimension ratio (slenderness ratio L_e / b) for short compression columns shall not exceed 12.
1.5 The slenderness ratio (L_e / b) for any slender column shall strictly not exceed maximum 60.
1.6 Minimum design eccentricity (e_min) for column axial loading shall be calculated as (L / 500) + (D / 30), subject to a minimum of 20 millimeters.

2.0 LONGITUDINAL REINFORCEMENT REQUIREMENTS
2.1 The minimum cross-sectional area of longitudinal reinforcement in columns shall be at least 0.8 percent of gross cross-sectional area (Ag).
2.2 The maximum cross-sectional area of longitudinal reinforcement in columns shall not exceed 6.0 percent of gross cross-sectional area (Ag) for un-lapped sections.
2.3 Where longitudinal rebar is lap-spliced at column base, maximum reinforcement percentage shall not exceed 4.0 percent of gross column area.
2.4 A rectangular column shall have a minimum of 4 longitudinal main bars, and circular columns shall feature at least 6 main longitudinal bars.
2.5 The minimum diameter of longitudinal main reinforcing bars in structural columns shall not be less than 12 millimeters.

3.0 TRANSVERSE REINFORCEMENT AND TIES
3.1 The diameter of lateral ties in columns shall be at least 1/4th the diameter of the largest longitudinal bar, and not less than 8 millimeters.
3.2 Maximum pitch/spacing of lateral ties shall not exceed the smallest lateral column dimension, 16 times smallest main bar diameter, or 300 millimeters, whichever is least.
3.3 For helical columns, the ratio of volume of helical reinforcement to volume of concrete core (V_h / V_c) shall be at least 0.45 * (Ag/Ac - 1) * (fck/fy).
3.4 Helical tie pitch shall not be greater than 75 millimeters, nor less than 25 millimeters or 3 times the diameter of helical bar.
3.5 In seismic confinement zones at column ends, maximum tie spacing shall not exceed 100 millimeters or 1/4th of column minimum cross-sectional dimension.

4.0 CONCRETE COVER AND SPACING
4.1 The nominal clear concrete cover to lateral ties in columns shall be a minimum of 40 millimeters.
4.2 For columns with cross-section less than 200 mm and using 12 mm main bars, nominal clear cover may be reduced to a minimum of 25 millimeters.
4.3 Clear distance between longitudinal vertical bars shall be at least the bar diameter or 40 millimeters, whichever is greater.
4.4 Clear concrete cover tolerance during column rebar cage alignment shall not exceed +/- 5 millimeters.
