PROJECT SPECIFICATION: MASONRY WORKS AND LINTELS
SECTION 04 20 00 - UNIT MASONRY CONSTRUCTION
STANDARD COMPLIANCE: IS 1077 / IS 2185 / IS 2250 / IS 1905

1. BLOCK AND BRICK DIMENSIONS
Clause 1.1: Autoclaved Aerated Concrete (AAC) blocks shall have standard length of 600 mm, height of 200 mm, and thickness of 150 mm or 200 mm per IS 2185 Part 3.
Clause 1.2: Concrete solid masonry blocks shall have a minimum compressive strength of 7.5 N/mm² at 28 days.
Clause 1.3: AAC block dry density shall be within the range of 550 kg/m³ to 650 kg/m³.
Clause 1.4: Common burnt clay bricks shall have standard nominal dimensions of 190 mm x 90 mm x 90 mm and minimum compressive strength of 10.0 N/mm² per IS 1077.

2. MORTAR MIX RATIOS AND STRENGTH
Clause 2.1: Cement-sand mortar mix ratio for load-bearing brick masonry walls shall be 1:4 (1 part cement to 4 parts coarse sand by volume) per IS 2250.
Clause 2.2: Cement-sand mortar mix ratio for non-load-bearing 115 mm internal partition walls shall be 1:3 with polymer bonding additives.
Clause 2.3: Compressive strength of 1:4 masonry mortar at 28 days shall be at least 7.5 N/mm².

3. WALL THICKNESS AND SLENDERNESS
Clause 3.1: External perimeter masonry load-bearing walls shall have a minimum nominal thickness of 230 mm (or 200 mm for AAC blocks).
Clause 3.2: Internal non-load-bearing partition walls shall have a minimum nominal thickness of 115 mm.
Clause 3.3: Slenderness ratio of load-bearing masonry walls shall not exceed 27 per IS 1905 structural safety guidelines.

4. MASONRY CURING REQUIREMENTS
Clause 4.1: Clay brick and fly ash block masonry work shall undergo continuous wet curing for a minimum period of 7 days.
Clause 4.2: Cement mortar joints in AAC block work laid with thin-bed adhesive shall be cured by fine water spray for at least 3 days.
Clause 4.3: Masonry work exposed to direct sunlight in hot weather (>40°C) must be kept continuously moist for at least 10 days.

5. JOINT THICKNESS AND BOND PATTERNS
Clause 5.1: Mortar bed joint thickness for clay brickwork shall be maintained at a uniform thickness of 10 mm (minimum 8 mm, maximum 12 mm).
Clause 5.2: Thin-bed adhesive joint thickness for AAC block masonry shall not exceed 3.0 mm.
Clause 5.3: Brickwork shall be laid in English Bond pattern, ensuring vertical head joints in alternate courses overlap by at least 50 mm.

6. WALL REINFORCEMENT AND TIE BEAMS
Clause 6.1: Half-brick thick (115 mm) walls shall incorporate continuous 2 nos. 8 mm diameter Fe500 rebar reinforcement embedded in 1:3 mortar every 4th course (at maximum vertical spacing of 600 mm).
Clause 6.2: RCC sill bands of 75 mm thickness with 2 nos. 10 mm rebar shall be provided at window sill level in all partition walls.

7. REINFORCED CONCRETE LINTELS
Clause 7.1: Reinforced concrete lintels over wall openings up to 1.5 meters clear span shall have a minimum depth of 150 mm.
Clause 7.2: Reinforced concrete lintels over openings exceeding 1.5 meters span shall have a minimum bearing length of 230 mm on each side support.
Clause 7.3: Reinforced concrete lintel projection beyond door/window openings shall be at least 150 mm or height of lintel, whichever is greater.
