ELECTRICAL INSTALLATION SPECIFICATION - SECTION 800: BUILDING ELECTRICAL SYSTEMS

1.0 WIRING, CONDUCTORS, AND CONDUITS
1.1 All light circuit conductors shall utilize FRLS copper wire with a minimum conductor cross-sectional area of at least 1.5 sq mm.
1.2 Power socket outlets (16A) shall be wired with FRLS copper conductors having a minimum cross-sectional area of not less than 4.0 sq mm.
1.3 Air conditioner and heavy motor circuits shall use copper conductors with a minimum size of 6.0 sq mm.
1.4 Rigid heavy-duty PVC electrical conduits buried in slabs or walls shall have a minimum outer diameter of at least 20 millimeters and minimum wall thickness of 2.0 mm.
1.5 Maximum fill ratio of wires inside any electrical conduit shall not exceed 40 percent of total internal cross-sectional conduit area.

2.0 DISTRIBUTION BOARDS AND CIRCUIT PROTECTION
2.1 Main Distribution Boards (MDB) shall be enclosed in sheet steel cabinets with a minimum IP rating of at least IP42 for indoor use and IP65 for outdoor locations.
2.2 Earth Leakage Circuit Breakers (ELCB / RCCB) with a trip sensitivity rating of maximum 30 milliamperes (30 mA) must be installed on all residential socket circuits.
2.3 Miniature Circuit Breakers (MCB) for lighting circuits shall have a maximum current rating of 10 Amperes with a short-circuit breaking capacity of at least 10 kA.
2.4 Dedicated 16 Ampere MCBs shall protect individual power socket loops serving kitchen appliances.
2.5 Voltage drop between the main incoming supply meter and the furthest socket outlet terminal shall not exceed a maximum of 3.0 percent of normal supply voltage.

3.0 EARTHING AND GROUNDING SYSTEM
3.1 Copper plate earthing electrode dimensions shall be a minimum of 600 mm x 600 mm with a minimum plate thickness of at least 3.0 millimeters.
3.2 Pipe earthing electrodes shall consist of G.I. pipe of minimum internal diameter of 38 millimeters and a minimum pipe length of 2.5 meters.
3.3 The total electrical ground loop resistance of the main earthing system shall strictly not exceed maximum 1.0 Ohm.
3.4 Main earthing conductors connecting DB boards to grounding electrodes shall be at least 14 SWG (2.0 mm diameter) bare copper wire or 25x3 mm G.I. strip.
3.5 Minimum distance between two separate ground earthing electrodes shall not be less than 2.0 meters.

4.0 LIGHTING AND ILLUMINATION STANDARDS
4.1 General office working areas shall maintain a minimum maintained illuminance level of 300 Lux measured at desk height (0.75 m above floor level).
4.2 Residential living rooms shall be provided with a minimum average light level of at least 150 Lux.
4.3 Staircases and emergency escape corridors must maintain a minimum light intensity of at least 50 Lux at floor level.

5.0 SWITCHBOARD AND SOCKET OUTLET HEIGHTS
5.1 Standard light switchboards shall be installed at a height of minimum 1.2 meters and maximum 1.3 meters above finished floor level.
5.2 Convenience 6A socket outlets in habitable rooms shall be placed at a minimum height of 300 millimeters above finished floor level.
5.3 Kitchen countertop power socket outlets shall be installed at a minimum height of 200 millimeters above working counter surface level.
5.4 Bathroom mirror light points and shaver sockets must maintain a clear distance of at least 600 millimeters from water tap outlets.
