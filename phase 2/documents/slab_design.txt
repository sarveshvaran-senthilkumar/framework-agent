REINFORCED CONCRETE SLAB DESIGN SPECIFICATION - SECTION 600: FLOOR AND ROOF SLABS

1.0 SLAB THICKNESS AND SPAN-TO-DEPTH RATIOS
1.1 The minimum total thickness for residential reinforced concrete floor slabs shall be at least 110 millimeters.
1.2 The minimum thickness for roof slabs subjected to solar insulation and thermal stresses shall be at least 125 millimeters.
1.3 The basic span-to-effective-depth ratio (L/d) for simply supported one-way slabs shall not exceed maximum 20.
1.4 The basic span-to-effective-depth ratio (L/d) for continuous one-way slabs shall not exceed maximum 26.
1.5 For two-way simply supported slabs with mild steel, the span-to-overall-depth ratio (L/D) shall not exceed maximum 35 for spans up to 3.5 meters.
1.6 For two-way continuous slabs with Fe 500 rebar, the span-to-overall-depth ratio (L/D) shall not exceed maximum 40 for spans up to 3.5 meters.

2.0 MAIN AND DISTRIBUTION REINFORCEMENT
2.1 Minimum percentage of distribution steel in slabs using Fe 500 grade deformed bars shall be at least 0.12 percent of gross cross-sectional area.
2.2 Minimum percentage of main reinforcement in slabs using mild steel bars shall be at least 0.15 percent of total slab area.
2.3 Maximum percentage of main tension reinforcement in floor slabs shall not exceed 2.0 percent of gross cross-sectional area.
2.4 The minimum diameter of main reinforcing bars used in floor slabs shall not be less than 8 millimeters.
2.5 The maximum diameter of any slab reinforcement bar shall not exceed 1/8th of the total slab thickness (D / 8).
2.6 Maximum spacing of main tension reinforcement bars in slabs shall not exceed 3 times the effective depth (3d) or 300 millimeters, whichever is smaller.
2.7 Maximum spacing of distribution rebar in slabs shall not exceed 4 times the effective depth (4d) or 450 millimeters, whichever is less.

3.0 TWO-WAY SLAB EDGE STRIPS AND TENSION RESTRAINT
3.1 Torsional reinforcement shall be provided at corners of two-way slabs where edges are restrained against lifting, with an area of at least 75 percent of maximum mid-span tension steel.
3.2 Corner torsional rebar mats shall extend from edges for a minimum distance of 1/5th of the shorter span length (Lx / 5).
3.3 The slab middle strip width for two-way panel calculations shall equal a minimum of 3/4th of the total panel width.
3.4 The slab edge strips shall occupy a maximum width of 1/8th of the panel dimension on each side.

4.0 DEFLECTION LIMITS AND COVER REQUIREMENTS
4.1 The long-term deflection of floor slabs due to all sustained loads including creep and shrinkage shall not exceed Span / 250.
4.2 Maximum deflection occurring after installation of internal non-structural masonry partitions shall not exceed Span / 350 or 20 millimeters, whichever is smaller.
4.3 The nominal clear concrete cover for interior slab reinforcement under mild exposure shall be a minimum of 15 millimeters.
4.4 Clear cover for external cantilever slab soffits subjected to moderate atmospheric exposure shall be at least 20 millimeters.
