REINFORCED CONCRETE BEAM DESIGN SPECIFICATION - SECTION 400: STRUCTURAL BEAMS

1.0 GEOMETRIC CONSTRAINTS AND DIMENSIONAL LIMITS
1.1 The minimum overall web width for structural reinforced concrete beams shall be at least 200 millimeters.
1.2 The width-to-depth ratio (b/D) for rectangular beams shall not be less than 0.30 to ensure lateral stability.
1.3 The effective span to effective depth ratio (L/d) for simply supported beams shall not exceed a maximum of 20 for spans up to 10 meters.
1.4 For continuous beams, the effective span to effective depth ratio (L/d) shall not exceed maximum 26.
1.5 For cantilever beams, the effective span to effective depth ratio (L/d) shall strictly not exceed maximum 7.
1.6 Total overall beam depth shall not exceed 1/10th of the span length for standard gravity load frames.

2.0 LONGITUDINAL REINFORCEMENT REQUIREMENTS
2.1 The minimum area of tension reinforcement in beams (As_min) shall not be less than (0.85 * b * d / fy), where fy is yield strength in N/mm².
2.2 The maximum percentage of longitudinal tension reinforcement in beams shall not exceed 4.0 percent of total cross-sectional area (b * D).
2.3 The maximum percentage of longitudinal compression reinforcement in doubly reinforced beams shall not exceed 4.0 percent of gross beam area.
2.4 Side face reinforcement shall be provided along both faces of beams when total depth exceeds 750 millimeters, with an area of at least 0.1 percent of web area.
2.5 Maximum spacing of side face reinforcement bars shall not exceed 300 millimeters or web thickness, whichever is smaller.

3.0 SHEAR REINFORCEMENT AND STIRRUPS
3.1 Minimum shear reinforcement area per unit spacing (Asv / s) shall not be less than (0.4 * b / 0.87 * fy).
3.2 Maximum spacing of shear stirrups along the beam axis shall not exceed 0.75 times the effective depth (0.75d) or 300 millimeters, whichever is less.
3.3 In seismic zone IV and V, stirrup spacing within a distance of 2 times member depth (2D) from column faces shall not exceed d/4 or 100 millimeters, whichever is smaller.
3.4 The minimum bar diameter for vertical shear stirrups shall be at least 8 millimeters for beam spans up to 6 meters.
3.5 Double-legged stirrups must be used as minimum configuration; single-legged open ties are strictly prohibited for structural beams.

4.0 DEFLECTION CONTROL AND SERVICEABILITY
4.1 The final deflection of beams due to all loads including temperature, creep, and shrinkage shall not exceed Span / 250.
4.2 Deflection occurring after erection of partitions or application of finishes shall not exceed Span / 350 or 20 millimeters, whichever is less.
4.3 Crack width at the tension surface under service loads shall not exceed maximum 0.3 millimeters for moderate exposure environments.

5.0 COVER AND END BEARING
5.1 The minimum clear cover to main longitudinal reinforcement in beams shall be at least 25 millimeters or bar diameter, whichever is greater.
5.2 The clear distance from beam bottom rebar to soffit formwork shall maintain a tolerance not exceeding +/- 3 millimeters.
5.3 End bearing length of beams resting on masonry walls shall be a minimum of 200 millimeters or full beam depth, whichever is greater.
