PROJECT SPECIFICATION: FIRE SAFETY AND PROTECTION CODE
SECTION 08 90 00 - BUILDING FIRE SAFETY & MEANS OF Egress
STANDARD COMPLIANCE: IS 1641 / NBC 2016 PART 4 / NFPA 101

1. EXIT DOORWAYS AND CORRIDOR WIDTHS
Clause 1.1: Minimum exit doorway width for assembly occupancies shall be at least 2.0 meters.
Clause 1.2: Minimum exit doorway width for residential and office buildings shall not be less than 1.0 meter.
Clause 1.3: Escape route corridors shall maintain a clear horizontal width of not less than 1.5 meters at all points.
Clause 1.4: Headroom along escape routes and stairways shall be a minimum of 2.2 meters throughout.

2. TRAVEL DISTANCES TO EXITS
Clause 2.1: The maximum travel distance to an exit door in a sprinklered building shall not exceed 45.0 meters.
Clause 2.2: The maximum travel distance to an exit door in an unsprinklered building shall not exceed 30.0 meters.
Clause 2.3: Dead-end corridor length in any building occupancy shall not exceed 6.0 meters.

3. FIRE EXTINGUISHER SPACING AND MOUNTING
Clause 3.1: Portable fire extinguishers shall be installed such that the maximum travel distance to reach an extinguisher does not exceed 15.0 meters.
Clause 3.2: Fire extinguisher mounting height shall be a minimum of 1.0 meter and a maximum of 1.5 meters above finished floor level.
Clause 3.3: Every floor area exceeding 250 m² shall have a minimum of two 6-kg ABC dry powder extinguishers.

4. SMOKE DETECTION AND ALARM SYSTEMS
Clause 4.1: Optical smoke detectors shall be installed at a maximum spacing of 9.0 meters between detector centers in open corridor areas.
Clause 4.2: Smoke detectors shall be positioned at least 0.5 meters away from any wall, partition, or ventilation supply grille.
Clause 4.3: Manual call points (MCP) shall be installed at a maximum height of 1.4 meters above floor level near every exit.

5. FIRE DOORS AND RESISTANCE RATINGS
Clause 5.1: Fire doors in exit staircases shall have a minimum fire resistance rating of 2.0 hours (120 minutes) per IS 3614 / NBC 2016.
Clause 5.2: Fire doors in service shafts and plant rooms shall provide a minimum fire resistance rating of 1.0 hour (60 minutes).
Clause 5.3: Fire door self-closing devices shall exert a closing force of at least 15 N but not exceed 30 N to ensure easy manual operation.

6. EMERGENCY LIGHTING AND STAIRWAYS
Clause 6.1: Emergency illumination level along escape routes shall be at least 10 lux at floor level during power failure.
Clause 6.2: Standby power duration for emergency exit lighting systems shall be a minimum of 90 minutes.
Clause 6.3: External escape staircases shall have a maximum riser height of 150 mm and a minimum tread depth of 300 mm.
Clause 6.4: Handrail height for exit staircases shall be set at a minimum of 1.0 meter and a maximum of 1.1 meters above tread nosing.

7. FIRE WATER PUMPING AND HYDRANT PRESSURE
Clause 7.1: Fire water storage tank capacity for high-rise commercial buildings shall be at least 200,000 liters.
Clause 7.2: Fire main pump pressure at the highest sprinkler head location shall maintain a minimum of 3.5 bar (0.35 MPa).
